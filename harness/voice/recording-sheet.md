## 5 Short Tips for Recording on a Phone in a Quiet Room

1. **Pick a soft, quiet room:** Close windows, turn off the fan/AC, and record near curtains, a bed, or a sofa so there is no echo.
2. **Hold the phone steady at chin distance:** Keep the phone mic about 15–20 cm (one hand-span) from your mouth, angled slightly below your chin so breathing does not pop the mic.
3. **Leave half a second of silence at both ends:** Press record, wait 0.5 s quietly, speak the line once at a calm, unhurried pace for a 67-year-old listener, then wait 0.5 s before stopping.
4. **Say numbers slowly and clearly:** Enunciate `1930`, `112`, `OTP`, `2`, and `5` cleanly with a tiny breath before them so they stay intelligible on a keypad phone speaker.
5. **Do one clean take per file:** Do not stitch takes; if you stumble, pause, take a breath, and record that line fresh, then play it back at low speaker volume to make sure every word is easy to hear.
